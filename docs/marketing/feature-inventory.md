# ReelBallers — Feature Inventory (August 2026)

> Source document for marketing material. Every feature listed is live in the product as of
> 2026-08-16. Format: what it does, then the value to the user. Audience: engaged sports
> parents (75%+ soccer) who film their kid's games and want professional highlight reels
> without learning video editing.

**Positioning in one sentence:** Upload your game footage, mark the plays that matter, and
ReelBallers turns them into professional, vertical, spotlight-and-slow-motion highlight
reels of your athlete — edited in the browser, rendered in the cloud, ready for family,
recruiters, and social media.

---

## 1. Capture & Organize

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **Browser-based, no install** | The entire editor runs in a web browser on desktop or mobile. | No software to buy or learn. Works on the laptop and the phone you already have. |
| **Full-game upload & cloud library** | Upload complete game recordings; they live in a cloud library shown as poster tiles, organized by month. | One safe home for the season's footage — no more hunting through camera rolls and SD cards. |
| **11 supported sports** | Soccer, Flag Football, American Football, Basketball, Lacrosse, Rugby, Volleyball, Hockey, Tennis, Baseball, Softball — each with sport-specific play tags. | The tagging vocabulary matches the game you actually film. |
| **Multiple athlete profiles** | One account holds separate profiles per athlete, each with its own games, clips, and reels; reels can be moved between profiles. | Families with two or three players keep everyone's highlights organized without separate accounts. |

## 2. Annotate — capture every moment that matters

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **One-click clip capture** | Scrub the full match, click Add Clip, and drag start/end handles to isolate the exact moment. | Turn a 90-minute game into a shortlist of great plays in minutes, at full precision. |
| **Ratings, tags & notes** | Star-rate each clip, add sport-specific tags (goal, dribble, save…), and a short note. | Your best plays are findable and sortable all season — the 5-star goals surface when it's reel time. |
| **Team / My Athlete layers** | Every clip lives on one of two layers: your player's moments, or the team's. The timeline shows both lanes; new clips inherit the last layer. | Track your kid AND capture teammates' plays in the same pass through the game — nothing gets lost. |
| **Teammate tagging** | Tag teammates by first name or nickname on Team clips. | Every family gets their player's plays — you become the team hero who films. |
| **Annotated playback & recaps** | Play saved clips back-to-back with titles on the video; per-layer recaps (Team Recap / Athlete Recap) with player filter chips. | Relive the game's best moments immediately — no editing required for the "watch it now" payoff. |
| **Game sharing with per-recipient scope** | Share the game by email, Google-Docs style — each recipient sees the clips meant for them (e.g., only their child's tagged plays). | Share generously without spamming every parent with every clip. |
| **Public game link** | One copyable link for the team chat opens an instant watch page — no signup needed. | Zero-friction sharing: the whole team watches tonight's highlights before bedtime. |
| **Claim & import** | A parent who receives a shared game can claim their player's tagged clips into their own account and build reels from them. | One parent films; every family can still make their own athlete's reel. |

## 3. Framing — make raw footage look professional

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **Keyframed action framing** | Drag and resize a frame box over the play; each adjustment sets a keyframe and the crop smoothly follows the action across the clip. | Camera-operator-quality tracking shots from static sideline footage — your player stays centered. |
| **Vertical / social-ready output** | Reels are framed for the formats people actually watch (9:16 vertical for social, plus source aspect). | Highlights look native on Instagram, TikTok, and phones — not letterboxed sideline video. |
| **Trim & slow motion** | Split the clip into segments and set the key moment to half speed. | The touch, the cut, the finish — slowed down so everyone sees what actually happened. |
| **Dim-check review** | A "Dim" background mode dims everything outside the frame so drift is obvious before you export. | Confidence the final reel is right the first time — no wasted renders. |
| **Transparent cost before export** | The Export button shows the finished reel's output length and its credit cost (1 credit per second of finished video) live, slow-mo included. | No surprises — you know exactly what a reel costs before you commit. |
| **Cloud GPU upscaling to full HD** | Export renders in the cloud with AI upscaling (Real-ESRGAN); you can keep framing other reels while it runs. | Crisp, full-HD reels even from cropped-in phone footage — with zero load on your own computer. |

## 4. Overlay — make your player unmissable

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **Automatic player detection** | The app detects players on the field and places markers along the timeline. | The tedious part of spotlighting is done for you before you touch anything. |
| **Player spotlight** | Assign your player at each marker and a spotlight follows them through the play; loop-playback verifies it stays locked on. | Anyone watching — grandparent or recruiter — instantly knows which player is yours. |
| **Manual override** | Turn tracking off and place the circle by hand whenever detection misses. | You're never stuck: every clip can be spotlighted, even messy pile-ups. |
| **Spotlight styles** | Choose highlight color and shape — Body wraps the player, Ground puts a glow at their feet — with an animated reveal. | Broadcast-style presentation that matches your club colors and taste. |
| **Text overlays** | Burn rich text into the reel: multiple fonts, position presets, drag-to-place on the live preview, timed to appear over a chosen span with a clean fade-out. | Titles, player names, scorelines — telling the story on the video itself, where social viewers actually read it. |
| **Thumbnail frame choice** | Drag a marker on the timeline to pick the exact frame used as the reel's cover image. | Your reel leads with its best frame everywhere it appears — tiles, share links, downloads. |

## 5. Athlete Intro Card — a branded opening for every reel

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **Animated intro card** | An animated title card opens the reel with your athlete's name, photo, and facts you choose (position, class year, team), in polished visual treatments. | Reels open like a broadcast package, not a raw clip — instantly more shareable and recruiter-ready. |
| **Card library** | Create cards once per athlete in a library; edit photo framing, subtitle, and facts; reuse across reels. | Set it up once, every reel benefits. |
| **Attach anywhere** | Attach a card to any reel or compilation from a picker with live preview. | Per-reel control: the recruiting reel gets the stats card, the fun one doesn't. |
| **Plays at every destination** | The intro plays on share links, downloads, and in-app playback alike. | Your athlete's name travels with the highlight no matter how it's shared. |
| **Parent-controlled & consent-gated** | Cards are parent-created with an explicit consent step; no birthdate, age, address, or school fields; full data export and deletion. | A safety story you can trust — and a differentiator worth saying out loud to privacy-conscious parents. |

## 6. My Reels — publish, organize, rank

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **One-click publish** | Preview the finished draft, then Move to My Reels to publish it in high quality. | A clear moment of "done" — the reel is now a keepable, shareable asset. |
| **Visual reel library** | Published reels appear as poster tiles under their game, with hover previews on desktop and instant play. | Browsing your athlete's season feels like a streaming app, not a file manager. |
| **Automatic compilations** | Reels are auto-grouped into Top Plays, per-game Game Highlights, and by tournament and month — no manual folders. | Season-in-review and tournament reels exist without you assembling anything. |
| **Reel ranking** | A quick this-or-that comparison game ranks your reels; top-ranked reels earn a Top Play badge and lead their compilations. | Two minutes of tapping keeps your strongest highlights front and center — and it's genuinely fun. |
| **Download reels** | Download any reel as a polished MP4 — with the chosen cover art and rich metadata baked in. | The file looks right in every gallery and app it lands in. It's yours, forever, offline. |
| **Download compilations** | Download an entire compilation stitched into a single video, with progress feedback. | The whole tournament reel in one file — perfect for team banquets and end-of-season gifts. |
| **Share everywhere** | Share links with rich previews; on mobile, share sheets upload directly to your favorite platform. | From "great play" to "posted" in seconds, on the device in your hand. |

## 7. Platform & Trust

| Feature | What it does | Value to the user |
|---|---|---|
| **Guided quests & video tutorials** | Step-by-step quests with narrated tutorial videos walk new users through their first reel, screen by screen. | The first reel is achievable on day one — no manual, no YouTube tutorials. |
| **Transparent credit pricing** | Simple credit packs; cost shown before every export; roughly a nickel per finished second. | Pay for what you render, know it up front. |
| **Auto-save & durable sync** | Every edit is saved as you make it and synced to the cloud, with conflict-safe versioning. | Close the laptop mid-edit; nothing is ever lost. |
| **Children's-data compliance** | Parental consent gates, full privacy export and purge, no biometric data collection. | Built for kids' sports from the ground up — a trust signal most competitors can't make. |
| **Cloud rendering** | All heavy processing (upscaling, spotlight rendering, stitching) runs on cloud GPUs. | Pro-grade output from any device — a five-year-old laptop or a phone works fine. |

## Suggested marketing angles (derived from the inventory)

1. **"One parent films, every family gets a reel."** — The share → claim → build-your-own
   pipeline (public game link, per-recipient scope, teammate tagging, claim & import) is the
   strongest word-of-mouth loop in the product and unique vs. generic editors.
2. **"From sideline video to broadcast package."** — Spotlight + slow motion + intro card +
   text overlays + HD upscale, stacked in one sentence, is the core value-add story.
3. **"Your athlete's season, organized by itself."** — Auto compilations + ranking + poster
   library speak to the retention/season-long value, not just single-reel creation.
4. **"Built for kids' sports."** — Consent gating, no-biometrics, privacy export/purge is a
   trust differentiator worth explicit copy, especially for the target parent audience.
