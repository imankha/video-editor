## Background — what was removed and why

`T5450-overlay-circle-and-loop.qa.spec.js` had 4 tests x 2 pointer contexts driving the
spotlight circle through `/overlaydiag.html`. They asserted a DOM contract that
**T5570 (`2567e90e`, "mobile move lever + stop drag competing with page scroll") deleted**:

| Removed spec assumed | Current reality (`HighlightOverlay.jsx`) |
|---|---|
| `[data-testid="highlight-move-grip"]` | gone. Desktop moves by dragging the circle BODY |
| — | `[data-testid="highlight-move-lever"]` — touch ONLY (`editable && isCoarse`), 44px, above the box |
| `highlight-handle-horizontal` / `-vertical` (rim) | four `[data-testid="highlight-corner-{nw,ne,sw,se}"]` |

Because the old ids no longer exist, `expect(GRIP).toHaveCount(0)` passed **vacuously** and the
next line then timed out for 60s — 8 tests x 60s of pure noise. They were removed on
2026-07-26 (commit `17e40530`) rather than rewritten, because `T5610-manual-override.qa.spec.js`
already re-proves their criteria against the current model, in BOTH pointer contexts, on the
same harness family. The criterion-by-criterion mapping is written into T5450's header.

T5450's **loop-button** test is unique, was kept, and passes — do not touch it.

## The actual question for this task

**Only add what T5610 does not already cover.** Read T5610 first and be honest about overlap;
a second copy of T5610 is worse than nothing. As of 2026-07-26 T5610 covers:

- test 1 — tap inside enters edit, tap again dismisses, tracking stays ON (gating)
- test 2 — a move drag while editing MOVES the circle and does NOT seek/play (move + no-scrub)
- test 3 — tap outside does not enter edit, drives tap-nav
- test 4 — toggle tracking OFF = free edit, no tap-to-enter target (the other gating half)
- test 5 — corner handles >=44px on coarse, compact on fine

Genuinely NOT covered by T5610 today (verify this yourself before writing):
1. **RESIZE actually resizes.** T5610 asserts corners are present and correctly sized, but
   never drags one. The removed test 2 asserted a rim-handle drag WIDENED the ellipse
   (`wAfter - wBefore > 20`). Corner-drag -> dimension change is real, unasserted behaviour.
2. **The coarse move LEVER moves the circle.** T5610's move test drags the BODY. The
   touch-only `highlight-move-lever` (the whole point of T5570) is asserted to EXIST by the
   Vitest specs but is not drag-proven in a real browser.

If, on reading, you find these ARE covered elsewhere (check
`HighlightOverlay.touch.test.jsx` and `HighlightOverlay.override.test.jsx`), then the correct
outcome is to **write that finding down and close the task without adding tests** — say so
explicitly rather than padding the suite.
