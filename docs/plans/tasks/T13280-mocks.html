T13280: see and switch between the videos of a game

Annotate, games with 2+ videos. Three options, each at 390 and 1440, for the real sequential game as measured (video 1 is 18.84 seconds, video 2 is 85 minutes appended at 0:18, both 1024x1024 so the player is letterboxed; playhead at 5:46 inside video 2) and for an overlap game (Main camera 0 to 60:00, Sideline 20:00 to 50:00, playhead at 30:00). Single-video games are untouched by all three. Nothing here writes; every tap is a seek or a camera switch.

Every option meets the two hard requirements from 2026-10-22: the active video is named next to the player at all times, and every tap answers with a visible change (selected state moves, the readout says "Now watching ...", and on an overlap the picture changes).

main footage, active main footage, not active (tap = jump to its start) extra angle, active extra angle, not active covers this moment: tap switches cameras in place added at the end (no usable filmed-at time) 300ms pulse right after a tap video boundary flag on the timeline

Names are what the app really renders: videoDisplayName trims to 14 characters, so "oct-5-2024-10-05" shows as "oct-5-2…-10-05". The desktop frames are 1440px wide and scaled down to fit this page; open on a wide screen to see them full size.

Why the current chips failed (for reference)

IMG_4412
oct-5-2…-10-05
Measured on the real game: at 1280 wide the chips sit at x=541 (w=96) and x=543 (w=92), at 390 at x=101 and x=104. Chip 2 completely covers chip 1, so every tap lands on video 2 and video 1 is unreachable. Tapping chip 2 inside video 2 seeks to 0:18 of the footage already playing with nothing on screen to say so; tapping it again at 0:18 changes nothing at all. Every option above gives video 1 a full-size target regardless of its 0.37% share of the game.
