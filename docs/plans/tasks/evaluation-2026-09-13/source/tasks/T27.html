R3 · N04–N05 · S04

Observed

Suspected / investigation

Investigate whether tracking exists elsewhere and whether detections identify one athlete reliably over time. Test occlusion, similar uniforms and camera movement; detection is not equivalent to tracking.

Selected decision and work

Use 6–8 formative parents initially, then iterate. For traffic experiments predefine meaningful effect, sample size/window and stopping rules from actual data; small sessions are not population proof.

  • Prepare tasks: find moment without supplied notes, make send-worthy highlight, leave/find/replay it, explain audience, then mark three more plays.
  • Collect active effort versus system wait, wrong turns, output preference, identity/action visibility, accidental drafts and audience understanding.
  • Run authorized sessions if participants exist; otherwise deliver materials and mark execution waiting for research inputs.
  • Plan comparison against repaired baseline and quality/annotation guardrails; report uncertainty and disconfirming cases.
