Observed

One clip passed through REEL, AI Focus, Clips and Highlight Reels labels; no reel was required. Saved preview promoted the game title/source timestamp instead of the clip name. Source and finished previews had similar labels.

Suspected / investigation

Labels may reflect different object models or reused components; inspect route/data contracts before renaming. Confusion is plausible, not a measured population rate.

Selected decision and work

Prefer one shared result destination with consistent entry behavior. Avoid adding independent viewers per route or forcing publication to replay.

  • Inventory existing entry points and route them to the same result identity/state contract.
  • Show poster/loading/error for ready media without treating transient black frames as permanent failure.
  • Retain source-game backlinks with timestamp and distinguish Watch marked plays from finished playback.
  • Handle missing/unavailable media truthfully; preserve saved settings and offer recovery appropriate to actual cause.
