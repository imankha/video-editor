## Selected implementation decision

Prefer one shared result destination with consistent entry behavior. Avoid adding independent viewers per route or forcing publication to replay.

## Work to perform

- Inventory existing entry points and route them to the same result identity/state contract.
- Show poster/loading/error for ready media without treating transient black frames as permanent failure.

- Handle missing/unavailable media truthfully; preserve saved settings and offer recovery appropriate to actual cause.
