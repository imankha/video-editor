Observed

Spotlight briefly claimed export was required while loading a newly exported video, then recovered. Progress advanced and all jobs completed, but an estimate was clipped. The account began with 88 credits despite walkthrough wording; upload cost two, six-second framing six and effects zero.

Suspected / investigation

Loading/dirty-state evaluation may race. Verify entitlement, ledger, fractional rounding, retry charging and storage rules. No render stall, double charge or purchase failure is established.

Selected decision and work

Use a dedicated neutral loading state. Changing only the warning text cannot fix an incorrect dirty-state predicate.

  • Trace entry into Spotlight immediately after successful framing.
  • Suppress export-required state until media/revision readiness is resolved.
  • Show real dirty/unexported warning only for confirmed unmet requirements; retain actionable media-load failure.

Proposed replacement copy

  • Loading your exported highlight…
  • Your framing changes need an export before Spotlight can use them. — only if confirmed
  • Couldn’t load your exported highlight. Try again.
