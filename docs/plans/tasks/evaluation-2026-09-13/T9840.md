# T9840: Make capture ranges and source clocks consistent

## Decision and verified mechanism (2026-09-13)

**The code was never broken. Only the copy was.** Established before scheduling this task, so do not
start by hunting for a range-calculation bug.

`AnnotateFullscreenOverlay.jsx:56-57` defines the capture window as
`DEFAULT_CLIP_BEFORE = 9` and `DEFAULT_CLIP_AFTER = 3`, applied at `:257`/`:260` and `:326-327` as
`max(0, t - 9)` to `min(t + 3, duration)`. That window **straddles the tap**: it is 12 seconds total,
9 of them before and 3 after. At the evaluator's tap of 0:03 it correctly produced **0:00 to 0:06**,
which is exactly what they reported as a bug. The arithmetic is right.

What is wrong is `displayNames.js:18`, `MARK_PLAY_HELPER: 'Captures the previous 12 seconds'`. The
window is not *previous*, it is centred on the tap, so the copy has been describing a different
policy than the one that ships.

Note there is a **second, separate default**: `useAnnotate.js:208` sets `DEFAULT_CLIP_DURATION = 8.0`
for `addClipRegion`. Confirm whether both paths should now agree, and make them agree deliberately
rather than leaving two numbers.

### Decided policy: 6 seconds before + 2 seconds after

**User decision 2026-09-13.** Eight seconds total, replacing 9+3=12.

The post-roll is preserved on purpose rather than going to a literal "previous 8 seconds" (8+0).
Parents tap **after** they see something good, so the seconds following the tap are the ones that
contain the end of the play. Removing post-roll entirely would require them to tap exactly as the
play ends, which is a worse capture policy even though it makes the word "previous" literally true.

### Work

- Change `DEFAULT_CLIP_BEFORE` to 6 and `DEFAULT_CLIP_AFTER` to 2.
- Change `MARK_PLAY_HELPER` to **"Captures 6 seconds before and 2 after"**. Constants and copy move
  in the same commit - they have already drifted apart once.
- Reconcile `DEFAULT_CLIP_DURATION = 8.0` with the new window so one policy is expressed once.
- Keep the clamp at both ends (`max(0, ...)`, `min(..., duration)`) and keep preventing zero-length
  saves near the very start or end of a game.
- Label source-relative and play-relative clocks distinctly, and preserve trim and playback context
  across fullscreen transitions (the second half of the original finding).
- **Coordinate with T9480**, which shipped exact trim entry and honest billable duration - the
  billable duration must follow from the new window, not from the old one.

The helper copy itself belongs to the T9860 sweep's vocabulary rules, but it ships here with the
constants, because a correct constant beside stale copy is the exact defect this task exists to fix.
