Hi there,

A huge thank you. You were among the first 400 people to sign up for ReelBallers, and that means a lot. This is a passion project for me, a soccer dad who just wanted better highlights from my son's games. Once I built it for myself, I showed it to other sports parents, and they all wanted to be able to use it, so I built this app. Every early supporter counts.

You're one of the few who made a highlight with the old version, so thank you for sticking with it. Since then I went back to work and rebuilt the experience to be much more intuitive, and added an in-app guide that walks you through each step.

We've also put together a [short tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ve2kwGGQL1o) that shows the whole flow, from uploading game film to downloading a finished highlight.

If anything slows you down, or there's something you wish ReelBallers did, just reply to this email and tell me. Even better, click the Report a problem icon ![Report a problem](https://app.reelballers.com/icons/report-problem.png) in the lower-right corner of the app, which sends me a full report. (On a phone, it's the Report a problem link at the bottom of the home screen.)

Or if you want hands-on support, [join our Discord](https://discord.gg/sXWam8j9QT) and chat with us.

One more thing: we're launching our social pages! Give us a visit (and a follow if you like what you see): [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reelballers1), [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@reelballers1), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@reelballers1), [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Reelballers) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/sXWam8j9QT).

If ReelBallers works for you, the biggest way to support us is simply telling another soccer parent about it.

Thanks for being here from the start.
