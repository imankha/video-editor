We just shipped a round of improvements, mostly about making your highlights easier to finish, find and share.

- **Pick up where you left off**: when you come back, ReelBallers takes you to the clip or highlight you were working on, at the same spot. Highlights and clips also have their own links now, so a reload or a bookmark lands you in the right place.
- **Finished highlights are easier to find**: finishing a highlight takes you to it, and your Highlights list shows it right away.
- **Sharing**: the Copy link button works as soon as your link is ready, and a shared link now shows a short guide for people opening it.
- **Download feedback**: you now see clear progress while your highlight downloads.
- **Sharpen progress**: the progress bar keeps moving to the end and gives you a time estimate.
- **Phones and iPads**: Annotate works in landscape on phones, the buttons no longer cover your video, and touch targets are easier to tap.
- **Fixes**: uploads that were slow or stuck on phones and iPads are more reliable, and several cases of Frame getting stuck on Loading are fixed.
