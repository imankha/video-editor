Hi there,

A huge thank you. You were among the first 400 people to sign up for ReelBallers, and that means a lot. This is a passion project for me, a soccer dad who just wanted better highlights of his kid's games, and once I built that, I just wanted to share it with friends. Every early supporter counts.

Our data showed that only a small number of you were actually able to create highlights. So I went back to work and rebuilt the experience to be much more intuitive, and added an in-app guide that walks you through each step.

We've also put together a short tutorial (the button below) that shows the whole flow, from uploading game film to downloading a finished highlight.

If you tried ReelBallers before and got stuck anywhere, just reply to this email and tell me where. Even better, click the Report a problem icon 💬 in the lower-right corner of the app, which sends me a full report. (On a phone, it's the Report a problem link at the bottom of the home screen.)

Or if you want hands-on support, [join our Discord](https://discord.gg/sXWam8j9QT) and chat with us.

One more thing: we're launching our social pages! Give us a visit (and a follow if you like what you see). The links are below.

If ReelBallers works for you, the biggest way to support us is simply telling another soccer parent about it.

Thanks for being here from the start.
